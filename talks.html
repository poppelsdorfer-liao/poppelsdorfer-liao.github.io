  • Verma modules for quantum symmetric pairs

    We construct Verma modules for quasi-split type AIII quantum symmetric pairs as induced modules. For this we use the Kolb--Yakimov star product to construct Cartan subalgebras uniformly in both subfamilies in this type. Then we construct a weak triangular decomposition of the coideal subalgebra into $U'_{<} \otimes U'_{\circ} \otimes U'_{>}$ via the Letzter map. This decomposition is weak because $U'_{\circ} \otimes U'_{>}$ is not a subalgebra. We show that these Verma modules have unique irreducible quotients and distinguish irreducible finite dimensional modules up to isomorphism. If time permits, I will discuss also the small example of $U_q'(\mathfrak{gl}_2\times \mathfrak{gl}_2)\subset U_q(\mathfrak{gl}_4)$. In this case $U'_{\circ} \otimes U'_{>}$ is in fact a subalgebra, and we introduce a family of magical operators to classify Verma modules that admit weight space decompositions. This also leads to an explicit weight basis of their irreducible quotients.
