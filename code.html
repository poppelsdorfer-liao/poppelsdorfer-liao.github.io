The regular representation of the quantum symmetric coideal subalgebras for $\mathfrak{gl}_2\times\mathfrak{gl}_2$

This is a SageMath research script for the quasi-split type AIII quantum symmetric pair associated to $\mathfrak{gl}_4\times \mathfrak{gl_4}$. It represents algebra elements in an eight-generator PBW basis and implements multiplication by rewriting products into that basis. It then uses those calculations to model Verma modules, rational irreducible representations, and tensor products with the natural representation. The final section sets up symbolic generators and sample vectors for computations.
