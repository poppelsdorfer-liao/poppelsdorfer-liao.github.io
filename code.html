The regular representation of the quantum symmetric coideal subalgebras for $\mathfrak{gl}_2\times\mathfrak{gl}_2$

available here

This is a SageMath research script for the quasi-split type AIII quantum symmetric pair associated to $\mathfrak{gl}_2\times \mathfrak{gl}_2$. It represents algebra elements in an eight-generator PBW basis and implements multiplication by rewriting products into that basis. Verma modules, rational irreducible representations, and tensor products with the natural representation are implemented using the same calculations.
