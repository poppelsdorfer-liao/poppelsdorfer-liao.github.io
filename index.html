talks

  • Verma modules for quantum symmetric pairs

    We construct Verma modules for quasi-split type AIII quantum symmetric pairs as induced modules. For this we use the Kolb--Yakimov \emph{star product} to construct Cartan subalgebras uniformly in both subfamilies in this type. Then we construct a weak triangular decomposition of the coideal subalgebra into $\negCoideal \otimes \csaCoideal \otimes \posCoideal$ via the \emph{Letzter map}. This decomposition is weak because $\csaCoideal \otimes \posCoideal$ is not a subalgebra. We show that these Verma modules have unique irreducible quotients and distinguish irreducible finite dimensional modules up to isomorphism. If times permits, I will discuss also the small example of $U_q'(\gl_2\times \gl_2)\subset U_q(\gl_4)$. In this case $\csaCoideal \otimes \posCoideal$ is in fact a subalgebra, and we introduce a family of \emph{magical operators} to classify Verma modules that admit weight space decompositions. This also leads to an explicit weight basis of their irreducible quotients.
